Why Do Mirrors Flip Left and Right But Not Up and Down?

It's one of the oldest puzzles in physics — and almost everyone gets the answer wrong. A mirror seems to swap your left and right, yet it never flips your head and your feet. So what is it really doing? The truth isn't about left and right at all. It's about front and back, a hidden property called chirality, and the quiet trick your own brain plays every time you look at your reflection. Once you see it, you can't unsee it.
