## Title
1,000 Families. Rent or Buy? (30-Year Simulation)

## Description
> We simulated 1,000 households — all earning the same $60,000 salary — over 30 years. Half rent and invest the difference. Half buy a home. Same starting point, two completely different paths. Watch what happens when a real recession hits, and see exactly where each group ends up.
>
> ⚠️ Important: This is an illustrative simulation built on long-run historical averages (S&P 500 and Case-Shiller home price data). It is NOT a backtest of real households, NOT personalized financial advice, and NOT a guarantee or prediction of future returns. Every person's financial situation is different — talk to a licensed financial advisor before making a rent-vs-buy decision.
>
> Sources used in this model:
> • S&P 500 peak-to-trough decline, Oct 2007–Mar 2009: Federal Reserve History, "The Great Recession" — https://www.federalreservehistory.org/essays/great-recession-of-200709
> • Case-Shiller U.S. National Home Price Index, 2006 peak–2012 trough: FRED (Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis) — https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/CSUSHPINSA
>
